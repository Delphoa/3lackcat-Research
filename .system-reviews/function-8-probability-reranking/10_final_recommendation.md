# Section 10 — Final Recommendation

> **Executive purpose:** State the recommended decision, its conditions, and the evidence that would support continuation or termination.

[Previous: Section 9 — Recommended MVP](09_recommended_mvp.md) · [Review index](README.md) · [Next: Section 11 — Sources and Traceability](11_sources_and_traceability.md)

## Executive Conclusion

Function 8 should remain on the 3lackcat roadmap as a **P1 core-value component** under conditional, evidence-gated approval.

The approved interpretation should be narrow: Function 8 is a transparent, calibrated, policy-gated advisory layer that recommends how eligible proposals should be ordered. It is not an authorization system, autonomous scheduler, verifier, or declaration of truth.

This distinction matters because 3lackcat's differentiating research claim depends on better allocation of a finite verification budget across multiple sources. The project should test that claim directly rather than assume that a more sophisticated scoring layer creates value.

## Recommended Decision

Approve development of the controlled MVP in Section 9 subject to these conditions:

| Condition | Executive requirement |
|---|---|
| Governance precedes influence | Scope, policy, source approval, validity, and exact uniqueness must be effective before ranking changes a batch |
| Simplicity precedes sophistication | Begin with a fixed, understandable, versioned policy; complexity must be earned by evidence |
| Claims match evidence | Raw source values, calibrated likelihood, priority utility, and verified outcome remain separately named |
| Authority remains separated | Ranker advises; ledger reserves; scheduler allocates and dispatches; verifier establishes outcomes |
| Privacy is preserved | Ranking records and explanations exclude candidate plaintext and unapproved context |
| Results are reproducible | Every material ranking and batch decision is explainable and replayable |
| Value is comparative | Promotion uses equal-budget comparison with fixed sequencing and the best individual source |

This is approval to test the proposition, not approval to claim that the proposition has been proved.

## Strategic Rationale

Function 8 addresses the coordination problem at the center of 3lackcat: deciding which of many independently generated proposals deserves limited evaluation capacity first.

Its potential value comes from:

- creating one decision surface across heterogeneous approved sources;
- reducing waste from exact duplicates and saturated candidate families;
- making cost, novelty, coverage, and uncertainty visible as operational tradeoffs;
- producing an attributable evidence record for research and governance review.

The basic concept appears feasible. The principal uncertainty is whether source evidence can be compared fairly enough to improve on simple sequencing. The MVP should therefore serve as a proof instrument as well as a product component.

## Conditions for Continued Investment

Further investment should require evidence that:

- the global order is at least as dependable as the strongest simple baseline;
- any reported improvement survives equal-budget and held-out comparison;
- calibration quality supports the language used in reports;
- explanations consistently reconstruct material decisions;
- uniqueness and reservation remain reliable;
- context adds value without privacy or coverage harm;
- ranking overhead remains proportionate to the value produced;
- replay and provenance remain intact as the design evolves.

Better accountability, clearer uncertainty, lower duplicate waste, or faster identification of weak sources may justify the component even without dramatic outcome improvement. These benefits should be measured separately.

## Reconsideration and Stop Conditions

Reconsider or stop the approach if:

- simple fixed sequencing performs equally well and the ranker adds no material governance or efficiency value;
- calibration depends on leaked targets, unsafe data reuse, or an indefensible evaluation domain;
- decisions cannot be reproduced or explained;
- a score can override scope, policy, validity, or uniqueness;
- sensitive content enters ordinary ranking records;
- live feedback changes behavior without a new version and approval;
- ranking cost and complexity exceed the savings or learning produced;
- Function 8 begins to acquire dispatch, authorization, or verification authority.

## Final Executive Position

Proceed with a transparent, fixed, replayable, privacy-preserving research MVP after its P0 controls are ready. Measure it against strong simple baselines. Keep likelihood, utility, authorization, and verified truth separate. Defer adaptive and opaque methods until the MVP demonstrates both value and control.

**Executive takeaway:** Function 8 should advance because it is central to the project's differentiating hypothesis, but it should advance as a disciplined experiment rather than an assumed source of performance gain.
